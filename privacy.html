StizIQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Who we are

StizIQ is operated by Wayne Patrick Harris Jr., doing business as WKW Technologies. Contact: stiziq33@gmail.com

What you submit

When you paste or submit text or documents into StizIQ, that content is sent to our AI provider, Groq, to be analyzed and returned to you. StizIQ is not designed to store your documents on our servers. Groq's handling of that content is governed by Groq's own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Some features, such as the Promise Tracker, save information in your own web browser. That information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and clearing your browser data deletes it.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number. Stripe's handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

Hosting

StizIQ runs on Render, and our website runs on GitHub Pages. These services may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) as part of operating their platforms.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell your personal information.

Sensitive information

Please don't submit Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, passwords, or medical records.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date on this page.
